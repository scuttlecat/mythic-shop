FAQ

When did this start?

I started recording these the day the new random mythic shop began: March 19, 2025. Previous Mythic Shop rotations were chosen by Riot and not randomized.

Does this include the "Featured" section of the Mythic Shop?

No, and they don't share the same pool. The featured section is more likely to include newer releases, while the skins and chromas in the random pool are very rarely updated. There was a confirmed update of the random pool in September 2026, where we began to see prestige skin chromas from 2025 and prestige skins from 2026.

Is this the entire random pool?

No, because it's random we still occasionally see new things pop up. Riot also updates the pool rarely. The newest confirmed items are icons/emotes from Season 1 2026 (Demacia), chromas for prestige skins from 2025, and prestige skins from 2026 (Prestige Veiled Lady Morgana.)

There are also some items I'm fairly certain Riot skipped over and are not in the pool until proven otherwise, like loot chromas and chroma icons for Fright Night skins.
